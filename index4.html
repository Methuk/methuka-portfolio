Roleplay & Moderation Knowledge

I have developed a strong understanding of common roleplay rules and moderation situations. I understand the importance of identifying rule violations accurately, considering the context of an incident and applying the appropriate action consistently.

RDM

Understanding Random Deathmatch and recognising situations where a player attacks or kills another player without sufficient roleplay justification.

VDM

Understanding Vehicle Deathmatch and identifying inappropriate use of vehicles to intentionally harm or kill other players without valid roleplay justification.

FRP

Understanding Fail Roleplay and recognising behaviour that breaks realistic roleplay or ignores the expected roleplay standards of the server.

NITRP

Understanding situations involving a lack of intent to properly participate in roleplay and recognising behaviour that negatively affects the roleplay experience.

LTAP

Understanding Leave To Avoid Punishment and recognising situations where a player leaves the server to avoid an active punishment, arrest or moderation situation.

RTAP

Understanding Return To Avoid Punishment and recognising situations where a player returns to the server in an attempt to avoid or interfere with an ongoing punishment or moderation situation.

SRDM

Understanding Server RDM and recognising inappropriate random violence occurring within the server environment.

Safezone RDM

Understanding the additional restrictions surrounding safezones and recognising inappropriate RDM or hostile behaviour within protected areas.

My Moderation Approach

When handling a report, I believe it is important to first understand what happened rather than immediately assuming who was at fault. I would review the available evidence, listen to the involved players and consider the relevant server rules before making a decision.

I understand that staff members represent the server and should remain professional even when dealing with difficult situations. I would avoid unnecessary arguments, communicate clearly and explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

I also understand the importance of knowing when to escalate a situation to a higher-ranking staff member. If a situation is unclear or outside my authority, I would seek assistance rather than make an uncertain decision.
